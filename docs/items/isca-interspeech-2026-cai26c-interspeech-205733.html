Beyond Mimicry: Constrained Exploration with GRPO for Joint Multi-Talker ASR and Diarization under Unknown Speaker Counts

Sep 30, 2026· Paper ISCA ArchiveASR Yunrui Cai, Dingdong Wang, Lingwei Meng, Xixin Wu, Zhiyong Wu, Helen Meng
Open source

Summary

abstract

Joint multi-talker ASR with speaker attribution and timestamps is particularly challenging when the speaker count is unknown and the output must follow a strict structure. We observe that Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)-only alignment of Speech-LLMs is brittle under dense overlaps, often leading to burst hallucinations and malformed speaker/timestamp tags. We propose a unified two-stage generative framework: (i) CoT-augmented SFT that infers the global speaker count before transcription, and (ii) GRPO-based constrained exploration guided by a Multi-dimensional Constraint-Aware Reward (MCAR) that directly optimizes permutation-invariant accuracy while enforcing counting, temporal, and structural constraints. On Libri3Mix and a Dynamic-Mix (2+3) set (speaker count unknown at test time), our model achieves 14.52%/9.24% cpWER and 1.95%/1.12% WDER, respectively, with GRPO yielding a 35%/54% relative cpWER reduction over SFT+CoT.
